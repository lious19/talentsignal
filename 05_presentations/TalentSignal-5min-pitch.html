Big picture · the problem

Staffing agencies find out last

Who it's for A staffing agency finds workers for companies — think of it as a dating app for jobs. They earn money each time they place someone.

The problem: they hear a company needs staff only when the job is posted publicly — and by then every rival sees it too, all fighting over the same opening.

Core features · the safety rule

The AI drafts. A human sends.

It can suggest, score, and even write the message — but it never sends anything on its own. A person always approves first.

Think of it like Your phone suggesting a text — it writes the words, but only you press send.
