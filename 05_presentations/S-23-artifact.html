Limits

What this run does and doesn't prove

honest, not tuned
fragile n

ml-ai sits at exactly the observation threshold (n=10). A family median this small moves materially on one atypical row — see the Product Designer example above, which is itself part of that median.

one row away

security is at n=9, one real Greenhouse posting from flipping out of curated fallback into measured. The threshold is doing visible work here, not just gatekeeping in theory.

by design

Leadership/management titles (~33% of real Greenhouse rows) have no family of their own and land in general-other, which is itself excluded from ever being "measured" — a blended median across unrelated director-level titles would be worse than not measuring it at all.

structural

retail-ops (530 real Lever rows) can never be measured under the current exclusion rule — every one of its observations is Lever-sourced. It rides the decision-026 curated fallback indefinitely unless that changes.

deferred → S-24

Lever's real median days_open (1,169d vs Greenhouse's 33d, ~35×) is almost certainly a field-semantics problem with Lever's raw createdAt, not evidence gopuff roles are 35× harder to fill. Excluded from every median computation until confirmed with a real client integration.

deferred → S-25

The Product Designer/AI false positive is left in the keyword list on purpose. Whether measured or curated basis actually predicts fill difficulty more accurately is a validation question, not a keyword-tuning one — that's S-25's job.
