Let me start with the simplest question:
what is this, and who is it for?

Start here · who this is for

First — what’s a staffing agency?

A company whose whole job is connecting businesses that need workers with people looking for jobs.

Think of it like A dating app, but for jobs — it matches companies that need people with people who need work.

They make money every time they successfully place someone in a job.

What is a staffing agency02
Their painful problem

They always find out last

Today, an agency learns a company needs staff only when that company posts the job publicly.

By then, every rival agency sees the same posting — and they all fight over the same opening, competing on price.

The agency that hears about it first wins the deal.

The problem03
So — what I built

A tool that spots which companies are
about to need to hire
— before they even post the job —
so the agency can reach out first.

What TalentSignal is04
Why it matters

Cold call → warm, early call

Without it

You see a public job posting. So did five rivals. You cold-call and compete on price.

→

With TalentSignal

You get an early heads-up before the posting. You call first, warm, while it’s still uncrowded.

Think of it like Noticing a friend seems off and quiet before they’ve said anything is wrong — you read the early signs instead of waiting to be told.
The value05
The one rule that runs everything

The AI drafts. A human sends.

The tool can suggest, score, and even write the outreach message — but it can never send anything on its own. A person always reviews and approves first.

Think of it like Your phone suggesting a text message — it writes the words, but nothing is sent until you press send.

The AI does the typing.
A human always owns the send.

The four releases09
The trust feature I’m proudest of

A record that can’t be faked

Every important action is written down permanently — who did it, when, and what changed. Nobody, not even an admin, can edit or delete it.

Think of it like A WhatsApp group chat — you can add new messages, but you can never delete or edit the old ones. The history stays exactly as it happened.

Proven live: a test tries to change and delete a record — the database refuses both times.

Tamper-proof record10
The part that’s actually mine

My job was to check the AI’s work

The AI wrote most of the code.
My job was to decide whether it was right.

It told me a story was ‘done’ more than once when it wasn’t. One example: a safety limit was accidentally applied to the whole app instead of just the login — a couple of page refreshes would have frozen everything, live in a demo. Every test passed; I only caught it by clicking through the real app myself.
