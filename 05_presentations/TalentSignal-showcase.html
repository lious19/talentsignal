The problem

Staffing agencies find out last

Think of it like A staffing agency is a matchmaker — it connects companies that need workers with people who need jobs.

Right now a salesperson learns a company needs people only when that company announces it — a job posting, a call around. By then three rival agencies got the same signal, and it's a price war.

TalentSignal spots the demand first — while the conversation is still warm and uncrowded.

The stack04
The governing rule

Trust Before Intelligence

The platform earns the right to be smart by first being safe, transparent, and reversible.

Safe

Nothing leaves the platform without an explicit human release. Passwords hashed, never logged.

Transparent

Every score shows its confidence and the reasons behind it. No black-box numbers.

Reversible

Every important change is written to an append-only trail the database itself protects.

The AI drafts and suggests.
A human reviews and releases.

The platform composes a tailored outreach package — but it is stored as a draft, delivered nowhere. Only when a human reviews and clicks release is it marked released, with the actor and timestamp locked in.

R2 · human-in-the-loop08
R2  // The tamper-proof trail

A diary written in permanent ink

Every pipeline move and every release writes a row to an append-only audit log — who did it, when, and what changed.

Think of it like A ship's logbook in ink — you can add a new line, but you can never tear out or rewrite a page.

The database itself rejects any attempt to edit or delete an audit row — even from an administrator.

Proven live: a test fires an UPDATE and a DELETE at the log, and Postgres refuses both.
