# Scope intake — questions to answer before writing any code

Answer from the Basecamp list first. Whatever it doesn't answer, ask the instructor.
Do not start Release 1 with unknowns in Section A.

## A. Blocking — cannot start without these

1. **What decision does the sales team make differently because this exists?**
   If there's no answer, the project has no client value and the demo will fall flat.
2. **What is "TalentSignal"?** A signal about candidates? About client companies
   likely to buy training/staffing? Define the unit of analysis in one sentence.
3. **What is the data?** Source, format, volume, who provides it, when.
   If none exists: am I authorized to generate synthetic data, and does the instructor
   accept synthetic data as valid for grading?
4. **What does "done" look like at end of month?** Software, analysis, or both.
5. **What are the four releases?** Does Basecamp define them, or do I propose them?

## B. Important — needed by end of week 1

6. Who on the Boston sales team is the actual stakeholder? Can I talk to them?
7. Current process — what does the sales team do today without this tool?
8. Any constraints: tech stack, hosting, data privacy, PII handling.
9. What does "story-driven" mean in the Basecamp title? Likely: each release is
   presented as a narrative (problem → insight → so what), not a feature list.
   Confirm the expected presentation format.

## C. Useful

10. Are prior interns' versions of this project available to review?
11. Grading rubric — what specifically is scored?
