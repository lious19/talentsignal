## Why this is a 🧑 HUMAN task — YOUR judgment owns the definitions
Ali flagged this human because the KPI DEFINITIONS are business decisions, not code
decisions. You own these (propose, log, flag for Ali since he's out):

- **Time-to-hire** — from WHAT to WHAT? The cleanest computable definition uses S-08's

These are the "top three numbers." Get them correct before anything else.
