## Story
As an agency manager, I want alerts on abnormal revenue outcomes and clients grouped by
need so I can act early and target well.

## Acceptance (Gherkin)
**Abnormal outcomes are flagged**
- Given a revenue KPI that breaches a baseline threshold
- When the detector runs
- Then an anomaly is flagged **with its baseline for context**

**🛡 TRUST — Alerts are tuned against false positives**
- Given a noisy metric
- When an anomaly fires
- Then a human can **confirm or suppress** it and the **threshold learns**, limiting
  alert fatigue

## Trust (TBI)
Anomaly alerts are explainable and human-tunable; **segmentation is advisory, not

## The two features
1. **Anomaly detector** — a revenue KPI breaks out of a baseline band → flag it, AND
   attach the baseline so the flag is explainable ("value was X; normal is Y±Z"), never
   just a bare "anomaly!". Heuristic-first behind the ScoringAgent boundary — reuse
   S-13's residual/outlier logic, do NOT reach for ML.
2. **Client segmentation** — group clients "by need." Advisory only: a grouping shown to a
   human, it must NOT drive any automated action/targeting (trust line is explicit).

## The judgment calls (propose, log in decision 025, flag for Ali — no silent assumptions)
1. **Baseline definition** — what is "normal"? (mean of history / rolling average / over
   what window). Reuse the S-12 analytics seed + S-13 history.
2. **Threshold** — how far from baseline counts as an anomaly (e.g. ± k × std dev, like
   S-13's >2σ outlier rule). A business decision.
3. **"The threshold learns" — the tricky one.** This must stay a TRANSPARENT heuristic,
   not ML. Suppressing a false alarm adjusts the band in a visible, logged way (e.g. the
   suppressed point is folded into the baseline, or that metric's k widens by a recorded
   step). It must be explainable out loud — NOT a black box that "just learns."
4. **Segmentation dimension** — what does "by need" mean? (industry / hiring volume / role
   type / demand pattern). Propose one, log it. Advisory only.

## Watch-points (for plan review)
- The flag carries its baseline (explainability), not just a boolean.
- "Learns" = a transparent, logged adjustment, not ML, not a black box.
- Suppression **persists** and honestly feeds back (record the human decision — connect to
  the S-08/S-15 audit discipline so tuning is itself recorded).
- Segmentation is advisory: no auto-action anywhere downstream.
- Reuse: S-13 forecast/outlier math, S-12 seed + hand-rolled SVG charts, ScoringAgent
  boundary (heuristic swappable for a model later without touching callers).

## Tests
Anomaly fires on a real breach and reports its baseline; the trust scenario (suppress a
noisy alert → threshold adapts → the same point no longer fires); segmentation groups
clients correctly; empty/short-history graceful (no crash); latency non-blocking.
