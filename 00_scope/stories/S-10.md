## Story
As a sales rep, I want existing relationships surfaced for an opportunity so I can ask for
a warm intro instead of cold outreach.

## Slice
`GET /api/opportunities/:id/relationships` → `RelationshipsSurfaced` → ranked warm paths
with a confidence level

## Acceptance (Gherkin)
**Warm paths with confidence**
- Given an opportunity and the relationship graph
- When the query runs
- Then candidate warm paths return ranked with a confidence level

**🛡 TRUST — A human validates before it counts**
- Given a surfaced relationship
- When it is shown
- Then the rep can confirm or dismiss it, and only confirmed paths inform outreach

## Build note (Ali's words)
> Model lightweight relationship edges, compute confidence-scored paths in the Node
> service, and render a confirm/dismiss review in React.

**Vibe-code it:** Relationships are noisy; a confirm step turns a guess into signal. Keep
the human in the loop.

## Trust (TBI)
Relationship inferences are advisory and confidence-scored; a human confirms before they
drive action.

## Keep it LIGHTWEIGHT — Ali's word
This is a "should," not a "must," and Ali says "lightweight relationship edges." Do NOT
build a graph database or complex traversal. A simple edges table + short (1–2 hop) paths
+ a confidence score is enough. Don't over-engineer.

## The human-in-the-loop parallel to S-09
Same DNA as the release gate: a surfaced relationship is a GUESS (advisory, confidence-
scored). Only after a human confirms it does it "count." Like S-09's release flag, there's
no real outreach yet — so "only confirmed paths inform outreach" means: the confirm/dismiss
STATE is recorded and confirmed-vs-not is a real distinction in the data, even though
nothing acts on it yet.

## Design points / risks
1. **The node model** — what entities have relationship edges? (agency users ↔ client
   contacts, former colleagues, etc.) Keep it lightweight and seedable. A judgment call —
   propose, log it.
2. **Path confidence scoring** — another heuristic scorer. Transparent, in a config object,
   marked PROPOSED, explainable (show why a path scores what it does). Same discipline as
   the confidence/match/opportunity scorers. Do NOT build an opaque or parallel scorer.
3. **Confirm/dismiss state** — surfaced → confirmed | dismissed. Stored. Only confirmed
   paths count. This is the trust scenario — prove with a test that an unconfirmed or
   dismissed path does NOT inform outreach.
4. **Anchoring the path to the opportunity** — the opportunity has a company; relationships
   are to client contacts. Linking opportunity → the graph may hit the same
   no-FK gap as S-09 (opportunities from signals vs. internal client data). Handle honestly.
5. **Advisory + confidence shown**, never a bare assertion (TBI rule 2). Every surfaced
   path shows its confidence and is clearly labeled a guess until confirmed.

## Tests
Happy path (ranked paths with confidence), failure, the confirm/dismiss trust scenario
(unconfirmed/dismissed doesn't count), idempotency (confirming twice is stable).
